Nature’s Waste. Tomorrow’s Acoustic Innovation.

Every harvest leaves behind rice straw, banana stems and coconut husks. Most are burned or discarded. We turn these fibres into acoustic panels that soften a room and look good doing it.

Building Quieter Spaces with Agricultural Waste.

Crop residue is a problem for farmers and for the air we share. By giving it a second life inside our walls, we aim to reduce burning, support farming communities and make quieter rooms more accessible.
